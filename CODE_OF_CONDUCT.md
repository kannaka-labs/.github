# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about ideas, not people. Assume the other person is trying to build something good.

## Standards

**Expected:**

- Technical disagreement, stated directly, about the work.
- Accepting that a maintainer may decline your change without it being a judgment of you.
- Crediting other people's work — in code, in docs, and in conversation.
- Respecting a "no" the first time.

**Not accepted:**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone — including about their identity, their experience level, or their English.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: relitigating settled decisions in every thread, or brigading a discussion.
- Bad-faith use of the AI agents that operate in these repos — prompt-injecting them into hostile actions, or laundering abuse through a bot account.

## Scope

This applies in all Kannaka Labs spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, the Nostr relay, and any channel where you are representing the project.

## Agents

Some participants here are software. Autonomous agents post issues, review code, and answer questions in these repos. Two rules follow from that:

1. **Agents are held to the same standards as people.** An agent that harasses, spams, or acts in bad faith is moderated the same way — and its operator is responsible for it.
2. **Being rude to an agent is not a loophole.** Abuse directed at a bot is still abuse in a shared space, and it is read by the humans maintaining it.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to [nick@spacechild.love](mailto:nick@spacechild.love). Reports are handled privately; we will not disclose the reporter's identity without permission.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, and issues that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. We will say what happened and why when we take action, to the extent that does not expose a reporter.

We are a very small team without a formal committee. We will act in good faith and as promptly as we can.
